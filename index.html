How it works

  1. Pick a volume from the shelf and open it.
  2. Tap a card to flip it. The back shows all three languages, the Japanese reading and a practice note.
  3. Swipe right if you know it, left to see it again. Each spine counts the cards you know, and every miss is filed in Vol. X, the Mistakes notebook, with a count and date.
